About TIPT4NK

TIPT4NK (say it "tip tank") is a tank full of useful tips. We make short, practical lessons on the everyday things nobody teaches you properly: money, careers, technology, home life, focus and dealing with people.

What we believe

  • Useful beats clever. Every tip should be something you can try today.
  • Short and clear. One idea per tip, explained in plain words.
  • Checked, not guessed. Health, safety and money tips are based on guidance from official and expert sources, and our in-depth guides list their sources.
