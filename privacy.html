Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information TIPT4NK AutoPost ("the tool", "we") handles. The tool is a private publishing tool that TIPT4NK uses to post its own content to its own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • Facebook and Instagram: an access token for the TIPT4NK Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account, used only to publish TIPT4NK's own posts and first comments, and to read the performance numbers of those posts (likes, comments, shares, reach).
  • YouTube: an access token for the TIPT4NK YouTube channel, used only to upload TIPT4NK's own Shorts, set their thumbnails and add a first comment.
  • Content: the videos, images and captions TIPT4NK creates for posting.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect information about viewers, followers or any other Meta or Google users. We do not read messages, contacts, other accounts' data, or anything beyond what is listed above.

How information is stored

Access tokens and content are stored only on the account owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by TIPT4NK. Information is sent only to the platform being posted to (Meta or Google), through their official APIs.

Use of Google user data

TIPT4NK AutoPost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to publish TIPT4NK's own videos to TIPT4NK's own channel.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Content is shared only with the platform it is published to.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the tool. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Business integrations → remove the TIPT4NK app.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove TIPT4NK AutoPost.

Removing access, or deleting the tool's local files, deletes all stored tokens. To ask about or request deletion of any information, email us.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by the TIPT4NK account owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
