5 myths you probably still believe

November 18, 2026 · Knowledge Tank

5 common myths: shaving and thicker hair, 10% of the brain, goldfish memory, lightning striking twice, Great Wall from space

Step by step

  1. Myth: Shaving makes hair grow back thicker. Truth: It doesn't. Shaved hair has a blunt tip, so it can feel coarser as it grows out.
  2. Myth: You only use 10% of your brain. Truth: Brain scans show activity across almost all of it.
  3. Myth: Goldfish have a 3 second memory. Truth: Studies show goldfish can remember things for months.
  4. Myth: Lightning never strikes the same place twice. Truth: Tall buildings like the Empire State Building get hit many times a year.
  5. Myth: You can see the Great Wall of China from space with the naked eye. Truth: Astronauts say it's very hard to spot. It's long, but narrow.
  6. Which one did you believe the longest? Save this for your next trivia night.

In short

  • Shaving doesn't make hair thicker
  • You use far more than 10% of your brain
  • Goldfish remember for months
  • Lightning can strike twice
