Be a better listener: stop topping other people's stories

November 18, 2026 · People Tank

When someone shares a story, don't jump in with your own. Ask one follow up question first, like what happened next, so they feel heard.

Quick steps

  • Don't reply to their story with your own
  • Ask one follow up question first
  • Share yours later, only if it helps

The details

Stop answering someone's story with your own story. They share something big. You jump in with yours. Ask one follow up question first. Try: what happened next? Or: how did you feel? Let them finish fully. Then share yours, only if it helps. They feel heard, not outshined. And they'll want to talk to you again.
