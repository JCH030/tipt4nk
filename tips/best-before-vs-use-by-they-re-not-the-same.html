Best before vs use by: they're not the same

November 24, 2026 · Life Tank

Best before vs use by: best before is about quality, use by / expiry on perishables is about safety; germs don't always change smell; follow after opening instructions

Step by step

  1. Best before is about quality. After that date, food may lose some flavor or crunch, but it's often still fine if it was stored properly.
  2. Use by is about safety. It's common on fresh foods like meat and dairy. Don't eat food after this date, even if it looks and smells fine.
  3. Why not trust your nose? Some germs that cause food poisoning don't change how food smells or looks.
  4. Opened the pack? The printed date may no longer apply. Follow the label's storage instructions after opening.
  5. Easy way to remember: best before, check it. Use by, toss it.

In short

  • Best before = quality (often still fine after)
  • Use by = safety (don't eat it after)
  • Once opened, follow the label's storage instructions
