Can your brain multitask? Why task switching slows you down

November 17, 2026 · Knowledge Tank

What feels like multitasking is your brain switching between tasks, and each switch costs focus. Group similar tasks and answer messages in batches.

Quick steps

  • It switches between tasks, and each switch costs focus
  • Group similar tasks together
  • Reply to messages in one batch

The details

Your brain can't really multitask. You answer chats while writing a report. Your brain is switching back and forth, fast. Every switch costs focus. Group similar tasks together. Block time for deep work. Mute chats during that block. Then reply to messages all in one batch. Fewer mistakes, faster work, and that report done before lunch.
