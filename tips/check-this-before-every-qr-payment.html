Check this before every QR payment

November 24, 2026 · Money Tank

Paying by QR? Before you confirm, check that the merchant name on your screen matches the store. Scammers sometimes stick their own QR code over the real one.

Quick steps

  • Look at the merchant name on your screen
  • It should match the store you're paying
