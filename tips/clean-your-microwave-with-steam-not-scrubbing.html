Clean your microwave with steam, not scrubbing

November 26, 2026 · Life Tank

Clean a microwave with steam: microwave a bowl of water with lemon or vinegar until steamy, leave the door shut a few minutes, then wipe the loosened grime

Step by step

  1. Fill a microwave safe bowl with about a cup of water. Add a few slices of lemon or a splash of vinegar.
  2. Microwave it for about 3 to 5 minutes, until the window steams up.
  3. Keep the door closed for a few more minutes. The steam loosens dried food and splatter.
  4. Open carefully, the bowl will be hot. Use a towel or oven mitt to take it out.
  5. Wipe the inside with a cloth or sponge. Most of the mess comes off in one pass.

In short

  • Bowl of water + lemon or vinegar
  • Microwave until it steams, then wait a few minutes
  • Wipe the loosened mess away
