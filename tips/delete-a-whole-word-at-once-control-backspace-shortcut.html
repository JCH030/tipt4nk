Delete a whole word at once: Control + Backspace shortcut

November 18, 2026 · Tech Tank

Hold Control and press Backspace to delete a whole word at once. Control + Delete removes the next word. Works in most Windows apps.

Quick steps

  • Control + Backspace deletes the word before your cursor
  • Control + Delete removes the word after it
  • Works in most Windows apps

The details

Control plus Backspace deletes a whole word at once. Still tapping Backspace letter by letter? Hold Control, then press Backspace. Each press removes one whole word. Wrong word three back? Press it three times. Control plus Delete removes the next word. Works in most Windows apps. Faster edits in emails and chats. Clear a wrong word in one press, not ten.
