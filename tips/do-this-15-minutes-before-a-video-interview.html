Do this 15 minutes before a video interview

November 25, 2026 · Career Tank

Video interview today? Test your camera, mic and internet 15 minutes early, and put your phone on silent. Tech problems are easier to fix before the call starts.

Quick steps

  • Test your camera and mic
  • Check your internet connection
  • Put your phone on silent
