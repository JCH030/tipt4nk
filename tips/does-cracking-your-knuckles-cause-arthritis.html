Does cracking your knuckles cause arthritis?

November 15, 2026 · Knowledge Tank

Research hasn't found a link between knuckle cracking and arthritis. The pop comes from a gas bubble forming in the fluid around the joint. If it hurts or swells, see a doctor.

Quick steps

  • Studies haven't found a link
  • The pop is a gas bubble in joint fluid
  • Pain or swelling? See a doctor

The details

Cracking your knuckles won't give you arthritis. You've heard the warning. Maybe from your lola. Studies haven't found a link between cracking and arthritis. That pop comes from a gas bubble forming in the fluid around joints. One doctor cracked one hand for about sixty years. Neither hand got arthritis. But if it hurts or swells, see a doctor. One less thing to worry about, and one myth you can correct.
