Gmail storage full? Find your biggest emails with one search

November 20, 2026 · Tech Tank

Type larger:10M in the Gmail search bar to find every email over 10 MB. Delete what you don't need, then empty Trash to free up storage.

Quick steps

  • Search: larger:10M
  • Delete the big emails you don't need
  • Empty your Trash so the space comes back

The details

Stop deleting old emails one by one. Gmail storage almost full? Your biggest emails are hiding. In the Gmail search bar, type larger, colon, ten M. It shows every email over ten megabytes. Delete the ones you don't need. Then empty your Trash. Trash still uses storage until it's emptied. Free up lots of space in a few minutes, without buying more storage.
