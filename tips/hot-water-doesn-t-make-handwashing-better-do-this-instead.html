Hot water doesn't make handwashing better (do this instead)

November 26, 2026 · Knowledge Tank

Water hot enough to kill germs would burn your skin. Soap and at least 20 seconds of scrubbing are what clean your hands, so any comfortable temperature works.

Quick steps

  • Water hot enough to kill germs would burn you
  • Soap + 20 seconds of scrubbing does the work
  • Any comfortable water temperature is fine

The details

Hot water doesn't clean your hands better. Most people think heat kills germs. Water hot enough to kill germs would burn your skin. Soap and scrubbing do the work. Use water at any comfortable temperature. Lather up with soap. Scrub for twenty seconds, backs of hands and nails too. Then rinse and dry. Cleaner hands, and less dry, cracked skin.
