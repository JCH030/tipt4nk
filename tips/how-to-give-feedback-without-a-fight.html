How to give feedback without a fight

November 20, 2026 · People Tank

SBI feedback: describe the Situation, Behavior and Impact to give feedback without starting a fight

Step by step

  1. Most feedback goes wrong because it sounds like an attack. You're always careless puts anyone on the defensive.
  2. Try SBI: Situation, Behavior, Impact. It keeps the talk on what happened, not on who they are.
  3. Situation: say when and where. In this morning's meeting...
  4. Behavior: describe what they did, not what you think of them. ...you cut Ana off twice...
  5. Impact: share the effect. ...and we never heard her idea. Then ask: what was going on for you?
  6. Who could you use this with this week? Save this for that talk.

In short

  • Situation: when and where
  • Behavior: what they did, not who they are
  • Impact: the effect it had
