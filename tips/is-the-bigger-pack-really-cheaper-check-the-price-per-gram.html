Is the bigger pack really cheaper? Check the price per gram

November 21, 2026 · Knowledge Tank

Bigger packs aren't always the better deal. Divide the price by the grams or milliliters for each size and compare.

Quick steps

  • Divide the price by grams or milliliters
  • Do it for both sizes
  • The lower price per gram wins

The details

The bigger pack isn't always cheaper. We grab the big size thinking it saves money. Check the price per gram. Divide the price by the size. Use your phone calculator. Price divided by grams, or by milliliters. Do it for both packs. The smaller number wins. Sometimes two small packs beat one big one. A few seconds per item, and you stop paying extra for the bigger box.
