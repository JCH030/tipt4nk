LinkedIn Open to Work: show recruiters only, no green frame

November 19, 2026 · Career Tank

On LinkedIn, turn on Open to Work but set it to Recruiters only. Recruiters can still find you, and there's no green photo frame on your profile.

Quick steps

  • Profile > Open to > Finding a new job
  • Add the roles you want
  • Set who sees it to Recruiters only

The details

LinkedIn can tell only recruiters you're job hunting. Looking while still employed? You don't want a public banner. Turn on Open to work, but show it to recruiters only. Go to your profile. Tap Open to, then Finding a new job. Add the roles you want. Under who sees it, choose Recruiters only. No green photo frame. Recruiters still find you, and your boss sees no banner.
