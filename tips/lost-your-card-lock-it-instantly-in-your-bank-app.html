Lost your card? Lock it instantly in your bank app

November 20, 2026 · Money Tank

Many bank apps let you lock or freeze a missing card in seconds and unlock it if you find it. If it's stolen, call your bank to report it. General tip; options vary by bank.

Quick steps

  • Open your bank app and go to your cards
  • Look for lock, freeze or block
  • Found it? Unlock it. Stolen? Call your bank to report it

The details

Lost your card? Your bank app can lock it. Card missing at the mall? Don't panic yet. Many bank apps have a card lock switch. Open your bank app. Go to your cards. Look for lock, freeze, or block.
