Never share your OTP, even with the bank (scam warning)

November 25, 2026 · Tech Tank

Scammers pretend to be bank staff to get your OTP. Never share it with anyone; hang up and call the number on your card or the bank's official site instead.

Quick steps

  • Your one time PIN is for you only
  • Someone asks for it? Hang up
  • Call the number on your card or the bank's official site

The details

Never give your OTP, even to the bank. Scammers pretend to be bank staff. Your OTP is for you only. Real bank staff won't ask for it. Someone asks for the code? Hang up or stop replying. Then call the number on the back of your card or on the bank's official site. Your money stays yours, even if they sound legit.
