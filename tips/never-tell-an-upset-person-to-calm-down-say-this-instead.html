Never tell an upset person to calm down. Say this instead

November 21, 2026 · People Tank

Telling an upset person to calm down usually makes it worse. Name what they feel, let them finish, then ask what would help.

Quick steps

  • Name the feeling: that sounds frustrating
  • Let them finish before you try to fix it
  • Then ask: what would help right now?

The details

Never tell an upset person to calm down. It sounds like you're dismissing them. So they get angrier. Name what they feel instead. Say: that sounds frustrating. Keep your voice low and slow. Let them finish talking. Don't fix it yet. Then ask one question: what would help right now? They feel heard. And a shouting match turns into a real talk.
