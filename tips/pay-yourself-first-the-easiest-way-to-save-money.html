Pay yourself first: the easiest way to save money

November 13, 2026 · Money Tank

Instead of saving what's left at the end of the month, schedule an automatic transfer to a separate savings account every payday. Start small and let it grow.

Quick steps

  • Schedule a transfer for every payday
  • Send it to a separate savings account
  • Start small, even 10%

The details

Save money before you can spend it. Most people save whatever is left over. Usually, that's nothing. Flip it. Pay yourself first. Save the moment your salary lands. Open your banking app. Many apps let you schedule a transfer every payday. Send a fixed amount to a separate savings account. Start small, even ten percent. You stop relying on willpower, and your savings grow without you thinking about it.
