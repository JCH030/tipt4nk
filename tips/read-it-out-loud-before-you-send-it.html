Read it out loud before you send it

November 26, 2026 · People Tank

Before sending a tense message, read it out loud as if you were receiving it. If a line sounds sharp, soften it before you hit send.

Quick steps

  • Text loses your tone of voice
  • Read the message as the person receiving it
  • Soften any line that sounds sharp
