Reopen a closed tab instantly (Ctrl + Shift + T)

November 13, 2026 · Tech Tank

Closed a browser tab by accident? Press Ctrl + Shift + T (Command + Shift + T on Mac) to reopen it. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Quick steps

  • Ctrl + Shift + T reopens your last closed tab
  • Press again for the one before
  • Mac: Command + Shift + T

The details

Closed a tab by accident? Get it back. It happens to everyone. You lose the page and your place on it. Press Ctrl + Shift + T. It reopens your last closed tab, right where it was. Press it again to bring back the one before. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. On a Mac, use Command instead of Control. No more digging through your history. One shortcut, and it's back.
