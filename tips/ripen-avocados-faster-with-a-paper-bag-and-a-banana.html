Ripen avocados faster with a paper bag and a banana

November 25, 2026 · Life Tank

Bananas and apples give off ethylene gas, which speeds up ripening. Put a hard avocado in a closed paper bag with one and check it daily.

Quick steps

  • Put it in a paper bag with a banana or apple
  • Fold the top closed
  • Check it once a day

The details

A banana can ripen your avocado faster. Hard avocados take days on the counter. Bananas give off ethylene, a gas that speeds up ripening. Put the avocado in a paper bag with a banana or an apple. Fold the top closed. Check it once a day. Ready for guacamole sooner, not next week.
