Say thank you instead of sorry: a simple communication tip

November 16, 2026 · People Tank

Instead of starting with sorry, thank people: thanks for waiting, thanks for listening. Keep sorry for real mistakes.

Quick steps

  • "Sorry I'm late" → "Thanks for waiting"
  • "Sorry for rambling" → "Thanks for listening"
  • Keep sorry for real mistakes

The details

Stop saying sorry when you're late. You walk in late, and your first word is sorry. Say thank you instead. Thank you for waiting. Instead of sorry I'm late, say thanks for waiting. Instead of sorry for rambling, say thanks for listening. Keep sorry for real mistakes. You sound respectful, not guilty. And the other person feels appreciated, not inconvenienced.
