Should you wash raw chicken? What food safety experts say

November 16, 2026 · Life Tank

Food safety agencies like the CDC advise against washing raw chicken, because splashing water spreads bacteria. Cooking it all the way through kills the germs.

Quick steps

  • Rinsing splashes bacteria around your sink
  • Cooking it fully is what kills germs
  • Wash your hands and anything it touched

The details

Washing raw chicken makes your kitchen less safe. Most of us rinse it. It feels cleaner. Food safety agencies like the CDC advise against washing raw chicken. Splashing water spreads bacteria to your sink and counter. Cooking it all the way through is what kills the germs. Then wash your hands and anything it touched. Fewer germs on your counter, and a lower chance of food poisoning at home.
