Sort your to do list into 4 boxes

November 19, 2026 · Work Tank

Eisenhower matrix: sort tasks by urgent vs important into do, schedule, delegate, drop

Step by step

  1. Sort every task with two questions. Is it urgent? Is it important? That gives you four boxes.
  2. Urgent and important: do it now. A deadline today, or a real problem that can't wait.
  3. Important, not urgent: schedule it. Planning, learning, health. This box is where real progress happens.
  4. Urgent, not important: delegate it or keep it short. Many interruptions and some meetings land here.
  5. Neither urgent nor important: drop it. Mindless scrolling and tasks nobody actually needs.
  6. Which box eats most of your day? Save this for your next busy week.

In short

  • Urgent + important: do it now
  • Important, not urgent: schedule it
  • Urgent, not important: delegate it
  • Neither: drop it
