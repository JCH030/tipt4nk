Stop just listing tasks. Schedule them

November 25, 2026 · Work Tank

Time blocking: put your most important tasks on your calendar as appointments, protect one focus block, add buffers and review at the end of the day

Step by step

  1. A list tells you what to do. Time blocking tells you when. Put your important tasks on your calendar like meetings.
  2. Start with one block a day for your most important task. Even 60 to 90 minutes of focus makes a difference.
  3. Treat that block like an appointment. Close chat apps and put your phone away until it ends.
  4. Leave buffer time between blocks. Tasks often take longer than planned, and that's okay.
  5. At the end of the day, look at what moved. Adjust tomorrow's blocks instead of starting from zero.

In short

  • Put your most important task on your calendar
  • Protect one focus block a day
  • Leave buffer time between blocks
