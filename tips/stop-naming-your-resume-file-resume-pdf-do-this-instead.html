Stop naming your resume file resume.pdf (do this instead)

November 26, 2026 · Career Tank

A file called resume.pdf gets lost among hundreds of others. Name it with your full name and the role so recruiters can find it fast.

Quick steps

  • Use your full name and the role
  • Example: Juan Dela Cruz Resume Marketing Assistant.pdf
  • Skip words like final, new or version two

The details

Never name your file just resume. Recruiters get hundreds of them. Put your full name and the role in the file name. Like this: Juan Dela Cruz, Resume, Marketing Assistant. Skip words like final, new, or version two. Your file is easy to find, not lost in a downloads folder.
