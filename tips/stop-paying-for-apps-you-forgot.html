Stop paying for apps you forgot

November 15, 2026 · Money Tank

Find and cancel forgotten app subscriptions on your phone

Step by step

  1. Free trials quietly turn into paid subscriptions. Small monthly charges are easy to miss.
  2. Android: open the Play Store, tap your profile icon, then Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  3. iPhone: open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  4. Cancel anything you haven't used in the last month. You usually keep access until the period ends.
  5. Then check your card or e-wallet history for other recurring charges.

In short

  • Android: Play Store → profile → Payments and subscriptions
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Cancel what you don't use
