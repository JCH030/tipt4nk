Stop saying but after a compliment (better feedback tip)

November 24, 2026 · People Tank

Saying but after a compliment makes people forget the praise. Swap it for and so both the compliment and the feedback get heard.

Quick steps

  • Swap but for and
  • Great report, and a shorter intro would make it even better

The details

The word but erases your compliment. People only hear what comes after. Swap but for and. Both parts get heard. Instead of: great report, but the intro is long. Try: great report, and a shorter intro would make it even better. Same feedback, kinder landing. They keep the praise, and actually fix the rest.
