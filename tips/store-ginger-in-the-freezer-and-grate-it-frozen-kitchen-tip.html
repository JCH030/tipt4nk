Store ginger in the freezer and grate it frozen (kitchen tip)

November 23, 2026 · Life Tank

Keep whole ginger in a freezer bag and grate it straight from frozen. No thawing or peeling needed, and no more moldy ginger.

Quick steps

  • Whole ginger in a freezer bag keeps for months
  • No thawing, no peeling, just grate it into the pan

The details

Keep ginger in the freezer, not the fridge. Fridge ginger shrivels or gets moldy. Put whole ginger in a freezer bag. It keeps for months. Need some? Don't thaw it. Grate it frozen, straight into the pan. You can skip peeling. The skin is thin and grates right in. No more wasted ginger, and fine grated ginger in about ten seconds.
