Tailor your resume for every job

November 21, 2026 · Career Tank

Tailor your resume to each job post: use the post's repeated skills as keywords (only where true), put matches at the top, keep the layout simple

Step by step

  1. Many companies use software to sort resumes before a person reads them. A generic resume can miss the words they're looking for.
  2. Read the job post and highlight the skills and tools it repeats. Those are your keywords.
  3. Use those exact words in your resume, but only for skills you really have. Never claim what you can't explain in an interview.
  4. Put your best matches at the top: in your summary and in the bullet points of your most recent job.
  5. Keep the layout simple: clear headings, a standard font, and no key details hidden inside images.

In short

  • Highlight the skills the job post repeats
  • Use those exact words, only where they're true
  • Put the best matches at the top
