The 24 hour rule for impulse buys

November 17, 2026 · Money Tank

The 24 hour rule: wait a day before buying things you want but don't need

Step by step

  1. See something you want but don't need? Don't buy it yet. Add it to your cart or wishlist instead.
  2. Wait 24 hours. For bigger purchases, wait a few days or even a week.
  3. When the time is up, ask: Do I still want it? Will I use it often? Can I afford it without touching my savings?
  4. Still a yes? Buy it without guilt. Not sure anymore? Remove it from your cart.
  5. Bonus: turn off sale notifications from shopping apps, so fewer flash deals tempt you.

In short

  • Add it to your cart, don't check out yet
  • Wait a day (longer for big buys)
  • Still want it? Buy it. If not, remove it
