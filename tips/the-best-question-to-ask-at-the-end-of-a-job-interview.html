The best question to ask at the end of a job interview

November 17, 2026 · Career Tank

When the interviewer asks if you have questions, ask what success looks like in the first 90 days, then connect their answer to your own experience.

Quick steps

  • Ask: what does success look like in the first 90 days?
  • Listen for their priorities
  • Link one to something you've already done

The details

Ask this question at the end of every interview. When they ask, any questions? most people say no. Ask: what does success look like in the first ninety days? Listen for their real priorities. Then link one to your experience. Say, I did something similar at my last job. End on that example. You show you think ahead, and you leave them picturing you in the role.
