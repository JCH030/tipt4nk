The interview prep most people skip

November 20, 2026 · Career Tank

Prepare three work stories before any interview. Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. One good story can answer many different questions.

Quick steps

  • Pick three real work stories
  • Shape each one with STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result
