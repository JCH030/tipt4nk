The thank you email to send after a job interview (3 sentences)

November 23, 2026 · Career Tank

After a job interview, send a short thank you email within 24 hours: thank them, mention one detail you discussed, and say why you're still interested.

Quick steps

  • Send it within 24 hours
  • Thank them, mention one detail from the talk, say why you're still interested

The details

Never skip the thank you email after an interview. Many candidates just wait silently. Send a short email within 24 hours. Thank them by name, and mention one thing you talked about. Keep it to three sentences. One: thanks for their time. Two: a detail from the talk. Three: why you're still interested. Double check their name's spelling. It takes five minutes, and keeps you remembered when they compare candidates.
