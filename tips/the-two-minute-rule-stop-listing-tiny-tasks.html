The two minute rule: stop listing tiny tasks

November 16, 2026 · Work Tank

The two minute rule, from David Allen's Getting Things Done: if a task takes less than two minutes, do it now instead of adding it to your list.

Quick steps

  • Under two minutes? Do it now
  • Longer? Put it on your list

The details

Stop listing tasks that take two minutes. Your list gets long, and small things pile up. Use the two minute rule. If it takes under two minutes, do it now. Reply to that quick message. File that receipt. Confirm that meeting. Takes longer than two minutes? Then it goes on the list. A shorter list, fewer things nagging you, and a clear head by lunch.
