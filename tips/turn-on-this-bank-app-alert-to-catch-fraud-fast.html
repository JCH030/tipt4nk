Turn on this bank app alert to catch fraud fast

November 26, 2026 · Money Tank

Many bank and card apps can notify you about every transaction. Turn the alerts on to spot charges you didn't make right away and keep track of your spending.

Quick steps

  • Open your bank or card app settings
  • Find notifications or alerts
  • Switch on alerts for every transaction

The details

Your bank app can ping you every purchase. Most people find fraud weeks later. Turn on transaction alerts for your cards and accounts. Open your bank or card app. Go to settings, then notifications or alerts. Switch on alerts for every transaction. Names vary by bank. A charge you didn't make? You know in seconds.
