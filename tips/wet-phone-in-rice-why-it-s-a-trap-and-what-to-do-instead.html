Wet phone in rice? Why it's a trap and what to do instead

November 21, 2026 · Tech Tank

Putting a wet phone in rice is a myth worth skipping, and Apple advises against it. Wipe it, tap out the water with the port facing down, let it air dry, and wait before charging.

Quick steps

  • Rice doesn't dry it faster, and grains can clog the port
  • Wipe it, tap it gently with the port down, let it air dry
  • No hair dryer, no cotton swabs, wait before charging

The details

Wet phone in a bag of rice? Trap. Rice doesn't dry it faster. And grains can clog the port. Apple actually advises against it. Here's what to do instead. Turn it off if you can. Wipe the outside dry. Tap it gently, port facing down. Then leave it somewhere dry with some airflow. No hair dryer, no swabs. Wait before you charge it. Your phone dries safely, with no rice stuck inside the charging port.
