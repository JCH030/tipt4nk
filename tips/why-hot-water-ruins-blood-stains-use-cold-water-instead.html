Why hot water ruins blood stains (use cold water instead)

November 24, 2026 · Life Tank

Hot water cooks the protein in blood into fabric. Rinse blood stains with cold water from the back, rub in soap, soak cold, and avoid heat until the stain is gone.

Quick steps

  • Rinse from the back under cold running water
  • Rub in a little soap, then soak it cold
  • No dryer or iron until the stain is gone

The details

Hot water sets blood stains for good. Most people grab hot water first. Blood has protein. Heat cooks it into the fabric fibers. Always use cold water. Rinse from the back under cold running water. Rub in a little soap, then soak it in cold water for about thirty minutes. No dryer or iron until the stain is gone. Your shirt comes out clean, not with a brown shadow.
