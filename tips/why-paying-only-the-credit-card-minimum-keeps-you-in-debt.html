Why paying only the credit card minimum keeps you in debt

November 18, 2026 · Knowledge Tank

Paying only the minimum on a credit card keeps you in debt longer, because interest is charged on the balance you don't pay. Pay the full statement balance when you can. General tip, not financial advice.

Quick steps

  • Interest is charged on the balance you don't pay
  • Pay the full statement balance by the due date if you can
  • If not, pay as much above the minimum as possible

The details

Paying only the minimum keeps you in debt longer. The minimum feels manageable. But the balance barely moves. Interest is charged on what you don't pay. Much of the minimum can go to interest. Pay the full statement balance by the due date if you can. If not, pay as much above the minimum as possible. Set a reminder before every due date. Less interest every month, and a card you actually finish paying off.
