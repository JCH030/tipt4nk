Why you should never put water on a cooking oil fire (and what to do)

November 23, 2026 · Life Tank

Water on a cooking oil fire turns to steam and spreads burning oil. Turn off the heat, cover the pan with a metal lid, and leave it covered until it cools.

Quick steps

  • Turn off the heat
  • Cover the pan with a metal lid or baking sheet
  • Leave it covered until it cools, and never carry the pan

The details

Water on an oil fire makes it explode. Panic makes people grab water. Water sinks under hot oil, turns to steam instantly, and throws burning oil everywhere. Instead, turn off the heat. Cover it with a metal lid. No lid? Use a baking sheet. Leave it covered until cool. Never carry the pan outside. Fire spreading? Get out, and call for help. One lid can stop a small kitchen fire before it spreads.
