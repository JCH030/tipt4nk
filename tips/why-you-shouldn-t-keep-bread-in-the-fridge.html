Why you shouldn't keep bread in the fridge

November 14, 2026 · Life Tank

Bread goes stale faster in the fridge because the cold speeds up how the starch hardens. Keep it sealed at room temperature for a day or two, or slice and freeze it and toast straight from frozen.

Quick steps

  • Room temp: sealed, 1–2 days
  • Longer: slice and freeze
  • Toast straight from frozen

The details

Stop putting your bread in the fridge. It feels like the safe move. But bread goes stale faster in the fridge. Cold makes the starch inside bread harden quicker. At room temperature, keep it sealed for a day or two. For longer, freeze it. Slice it first, then toast slices straight from frozen. Less waste, and bread that tastes fresh all week long.
