Windows clipboard history: paste anything you copied earlier

November 15, 2026 · Tech Tank

Press Windows key + V to open clipboard history on Windows. Turn it on once, then paste anything you copied earlier and pin the items you reuse.

Quick steps

  • Press Windows key + V
  • Click Turn on the first time
  • Pick any item to paste, pin the ones you reuse

The details

Windows can remember everything you copied. You copy something new, and the last one is gone. Press Windows key plus V. It opens your clipboard history. The first time, click Turn on. Now copy a few things. Press Windows key plus V and click any of them. Pin the ones you reuse often. No more switching back to copy the same thing. Paste five items in seconds, not minutes.
