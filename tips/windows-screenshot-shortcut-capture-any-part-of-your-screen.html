Windows screenshot shortcut: capture any part of your screen

November 17, 2026 · Tech Tank

Press Windows key + Shift + S, drag a box around any part of your screen, and it's copied instantly, ready to paste. No cropping needed.

Quick steps

  • Press Windows key + Shift + S
  • Drag a box around what you need
  • Paste it anywhere, it's already copied

The details

Windows has a screenshot shortcut most people miss. Still pressing Print Screen and cropping in Paint? Press Windows key plus Shift plus S. Your screen dims. Drag a box around just the part you need. It's copied instantly. Paste it into a chat or email. Click the notification to edit it. No cropping, no extra apps. A clean screenshot in about two seconds.
