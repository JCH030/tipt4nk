Work in 25 minute rounds to focus better ⏱

November 23, 2026 · Work Tank

Pomodoro technique: 25 minutes on one task, 5 minute break, longer break after four rounds; adjust the numbers to your rhythm

Step by step

  1. It's called the Pomodoro technique. Work on one task for 25 minutes, then take a short 5 minute break.
  2. During those 25 minutes, only that one task. Phone face down, chat closed. If a thought pops up, write it down and get back to work.
  3. After four rounds, take a longer break of about 15 to 30 minutes. Stand up, stretch, drink water, rest your eyes.
  4. Why it works: a short, fixed round feels easy to start. And the timer tells you exactly when it's okay to rest.
  5. Make it yours. Some people prefer 50 minute rounds with 10 minute breaks. The rhythm matters more than the exact numbers.

In short

  • 25 minutes on ONE task, then a 5 minute break
  • After four rounds, take a longer break
  • Write down distractions instead of acting on them
