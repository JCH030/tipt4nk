Your phone doesn't need to hit 0% first

November 22, 2026 · Knowledge Tank

Phone battery myths: no need to drain lithium ion batteries to 0% (no memory effect), heat is the real enemy, overnight charging is fine on most modern phones with optimized charging

Step by step

  1. That advice came from older nickel batteries, which had a memory effect. Draining them fully helped them hold their charge.
  2. Modern phones use lithium ion batteries. They don't have that problem, and draining to 0% often actually adds stress to the battery.
  3. The real enemy is heat. Don't charge under a pillow or leave your phone in a hot car. If it gets very hot while charging, take off a thick case.
  4. Charging overnight is fine on most modern phones. Many have an optimized or adaptive charging setting that slows charging near full.
  5. The easy habit: top up whenever it's convenient. Short charges during the day are perfectly fine.

In short

  • Old nickel batteries had a memory effect, lithium ion doesn't
  • Heat hurts your battery more than charging habits
  • Short top ups during the day are fine
