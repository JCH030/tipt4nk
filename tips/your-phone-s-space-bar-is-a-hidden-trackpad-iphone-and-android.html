Your phone's space bar is a hidden trackpad (iPhone and Android)

November 24, 2026 · Tech Tank

Move the cursor precisely by using your space bar as a trackpad: touch and hold it on iPhone, or slide along it in Gboard on Android.

Quick steps

  • iPhone: touch and hold the space bar, then drag
  • Android (Gboard): slide along the space bar

The details

Your phone's space bar is a hidden trackpad. Tapping on tiny typos misses. Hold the space bar, then slide your finger. The cursor follows you. On iPhone, touch and hold until the keys go blank. Then drag anywhere. On Android with Gboard, slide left or right along the space bar. Fix a typo in a second, without deleting whole words.
