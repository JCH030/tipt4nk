Your receipt might be fading right now

November 22, 2026 · Life Tank

Thermal receipts fade over time. Snap a photo of receipts for big purchases, so you still have proof for warranty claims and returns.

Quick steps

  • Thermal paper receipts fade with time, heat and light
  • Photograph receipts for appliances, gadgets and other big buys
  • Keep them in one album so they're easy to find
