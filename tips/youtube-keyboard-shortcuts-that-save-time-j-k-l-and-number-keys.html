YouTube keyboard shortcuts that save time: J, K, L and number keys

November 22, 2026 · Tech Tank

On a computer, YouTube has keyboard shortcuts: L skips ahead 10 seconds, J goes back 10, K pauses, and number keys jump to parts of the video.

Quick steps

  • L skips ahead 10 seconds, J goes back 10
  • K pauses and plays
  • Number keys jump: 5 is the middle, 0 is the start

The details

Press L on YouTube to skip ten seconds. Dragging the slider is slow, and you always overshoot. On a computer, use the keyboard. L jumps ahead ten seconds. J jumps back ten seconds. K pauses and plays. Number keys jump around too. Press five for the middle. Zero takes you to the start. Paused? Press the period key to move one frame. No more dragging the slider. You land on the exact moment in about a second.
